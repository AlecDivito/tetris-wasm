How to Play

Tetris is a puzzle game where you stack falling pieces (called "tetrominoes") to form complete horizontal lines. When a line is filled, it clears and you earn points. The game ends when pieces stack all the way to the top.

Desktop Controls

Rotation

Numpad1/5/9 or X or ↑ — Rotate Clockwise

Numpad7/3 or Ctrl or Z — Rotate Counter-Clockwise

Movement

Numpad4 or ← — Move Left

Numpad6 or → — Move Right

Numpad2 or ↓ — Soft Drop (accelerate)

Numpad8 or Space or Left Click — Hard Drop (instant)

Special

Numpad0 or C or Shift — Hold Piece (save for later)

Esc — Pause/Resume Game

Mobile / Touch Controls

Swipe Controls

Swipe Left/Right — Move Piece

Swipe Down — Soft Drop

Tap Right — Rotate Clockwise

Tap Left — Rotate Counter-Clockwise

Tap Hold Canvas — Hold Piece

Pro Tips

  • Use the numpad for faster rotation — it's more ergonomic for some players
  • Hold pieces when you see a good setup for a big combo
  • Keep your stack low and centered to maximize future moves
  • Clear lines as they form — don't let them stack up
